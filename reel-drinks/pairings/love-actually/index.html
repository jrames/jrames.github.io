5 real cocktails to drink with Love Actually, each one matched to the film.

01 Heathrow Arrivals GateMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for the arrivals gate at Heathrow, where everyone is hugging somebody.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Musicians in the PewsFrench 75 · Flute Gin, lemon and bubbles for a church wedding where hidden musicians stand up in the pews.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
03 Necklace in the CoatBrandy Alexander · Coupe glass Brandy, cacao and cream for a necklace found in a coat pocket and a different gift under the tree.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 oz Crème de Cacao
  • 1 oz Heavy Cream
  • Pinch Nutmeg, optional

Method

  1. Shake hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.
04 Outrunning Airport SecurityNew York Sour · Rocks glass Bourbon sour under a red wine float, for a boy outrunning airport security to say goodbye properly.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional
  • ½ oz Red Wine

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with lemon or orange zest with cherry.

Garnish: Lemon or orange zest with cherry

05 Cards at the DoorGin & Tonic · Highball glass Gin, tonic and lime, quiet and clear, for a confession written on cards and held up at the front door.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Gin
  • 4 oz Tonic Water
  • Limes (wedge), optional

Method

  1. Fill a glass with ice and add gin.
  2. Top with cold tonic and stir once.
  3. Squeeze in a lime wedge.
