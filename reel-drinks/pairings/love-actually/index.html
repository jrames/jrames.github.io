5 real cocktails to drink with Love Actually, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Romance: pink and sparkling

01 Window-Steam Winter WarmupMulled Claret · Mug Hot spice and citrus make it feel like a wool coat, a lit window, and a cold London walk in one glass.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Candlelit SparklerFrench 75 · Flute Its crisp bubbles and bright citrus match the film’s polished, hopeful holiday glow.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
03 After-Dinner Soft LandingBrandy Alexander · Coupe glass Creamy, sweet, and a little indulgent, it fits the movie’s cozy evenings and softened edges.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 oz Crème de Cacao
  • 1 oz Heavy Cream
  • Pinch Nutmeg, optional

Method

  1. Shake hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.
04 Red-Trimmed HeartacheNew York Sour · Rocks glass The wine cap gives a romantic, bittersweet look that suits the film’s mix of charm and ache.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional
  • ½ oz Red Wine

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with lemon or orange zest with cherry.

Garnish: Lemon or orange zest with cherry

05 Party Lights in a GlassRussian Spring Punch · Highball glass Sparkling, berry-bright, and festive, it has the easy holiday cheer of a crowded evening out.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Vodka
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Crème de Cassis
  • ¼ oz Simple Syrup
  • Top Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake vodka, lemon juice, crème de cassis and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a highball glass over fresh ice.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
