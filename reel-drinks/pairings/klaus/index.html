Klaus 2019

5 real cocktails to drink with Klaus, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Old-School Holiday ComfortEgg Nog · Collins glass Its creamy, nostalgic sweetness matches the film’s cozy winter warmth and old-fashioned charm.

Ingredients

  • 1 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 4 oz Milk

Method

  1. Shake an egg, sugar, cognac, dark rum and milk hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Snowfall in a GlassMulled Claret · Mug The spiced red wine feels right for a story that leans into hearthside color, cold air, and holiday glow.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

03 Quiet Night WatchHot Toddy · Mug This gentle, honeyed warmer fits the film’s bittersweet tenderness and snowy hush.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick

04 Cartoon-Weekender ClassicTom and Jerry · Mug The frothy, spiced richness echoes the playful, hand-crafted holiday spirit and classic-era feel.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

05 Firelight and FootstepsHot Brandy Sling · Mug Simple, warming brandy suits the film’s brisk winter mood and old-world setting.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg
