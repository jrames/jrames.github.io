5 real cocktails to drink with The Holiday, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era

01 Two-Week House SwapFrench 75 · Flute Gin, lemon and bubbles for two strangers trading houses for Christmas, an English cottage for a mansion.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
02 Lane Too NarrowMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for a snowy English cottage at the end of a lane too narrow for a car.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

03 Life as a TrailerRamos Fizz · Highball glass Gin, cream and egg white, fluffy and dramatic, for a woman who narrates her own life like a movie trailer.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Heavy Cream
  • 1 oz Egg Whites
  • 3 dashes Orange Flower Water
  • Top Soda Water

Method

  1. Shake gin, lime juice, lemon juice, simple syrup, cream, egg white and orange flower water without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Add ice and shake hard until very cold.
  3. Strain into a highball glass over fresh ice.
  4. Top with soda water.
04 Widower's Napkin TrickMimosa · Flute Orange juice and bubbles for a widowed dad who entertains his daughters with a napkin on his face.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz Orange juice
  • 2½ oz Sparkling Wine (prosecco)

Method

  1. Add orange juice to a chilled flute glass.
  2. Top with sparkling wine.
  3. Garnish with an orange twist.

Garnish: An orange twist

05 Too Much HouseCosmopolitan · Cocktail glass Vodka, orange liqueur, cranberry and lime for a Los Angeles house so big a guest from Surrey feels tiny.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz Vodka
  • ½ oz Orange Liqueur
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • 1 oz Cranberry Juice

Method

  1. Shake vodka, orange liqueur, lime juice and cranberry juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Garnish: A lemon twist
