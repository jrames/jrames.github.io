The Holiday 2006

5 real cocktails to drink with The Holiday, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Romance: pink and sparkling
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Sparkling meet-cute energyFrench 75 · Flute Bubbly, elegant, and bright, it matches the film’s polished romantic sparkle without feeling heavy.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
02 Brunch-in-the-sun pickMimosa · Flute Its sunny citrus lift suits the film’s easygoing, feel-good warmth and daytime glow.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz Orange juice
  • 2½ oz Sparkling Wine (prosecco)

Method

  1. Add orange juice to a chilled flute glass.
  2. Top with sparkling wine.
  3. Garnish with an orange twist.

Garnish: An orange twist

03 Clean-cut crowd-pleaserTom Collins · Collins glass Light, crisp, and breezy, it fits the modern, polished atmosphere and keeps the mood upbeat.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • Top Soda Water

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon and syrup briefly with ice.
  2. Strain into an ice-filled glass.
  3. Top with soda.

Garnish: Lemon wheel & cherry

04 City-romance classicCosmopolitan · Cocktail glass The pink cranberry-citrus profile feels stylish, flirty, and perfectly at home in a glossy modern romance.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz Vodka
  • ½ oz Orange Liqueur
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • 1 oz Cranberry Juice

Method

  1. Shake vodka, orange liqueur, lime juice and cranberry juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Garnish: A lemon twist

05 Bright patio sipPaloma · Highball glass Its tart grapefruit snap brings a fresh, lively note that feels relaxed and sunlit.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tequila
  • 1½ oz Grapefruit juice (fresh juice)
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • Top Soda Water
  • Pinch Salt

Method

  1. Add tequila, juices, syrup and salt to an ice-filled glass.
  2. Top with soda and stir.

Garnish: Grapefruit wedge
