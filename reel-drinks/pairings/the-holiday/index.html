01 Christmas Lights On LaurelFrench 75 · Flute Bubbles and gin make everything brighter, like a winter night strung with lights and bad decisions in Los Angeles.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
02 Snowed-In NightsMulled Claret · Mug Warm red wine and spice are for when the holidays land hard and the windows stay foggy all evening.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

03 Beverly Hills MorningRamos Fizz · Highball glass Creamy foam and orange flower water turn brunch into a polished little escape, even if your life is a mess.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Heavy Cream
  • 1 oz Egg Whites
  • 3 dashes Orange Flower Water
  • Top Soda Water

Method

  1. Shake gin, lime juice, lemon juice, simple syrup, cream, egg white and orange flower water without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Add ice and shake hard until very cold.
  3. Strain into a highball glass over fresh ice.
  4. Top with soda water.
04 Brunch Table ConfessionMimosa · Flute Orange and sparkling wine keep things sunny while everybody pretends the family calendar is under control.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz Orange juice
  • 2½ oz Sparkling Wine (prosecco)

Method

  1. Add orange juice to a chilled flute glass.
  2. Top with sparkling wine.
  3. Garnish with an orange twist.

Garnish: An orange twist

05 Westside Gift WrapCosmopolitan · Cocktail glass Cranberry and citrus bring the slick red-white polish of a shopping run that somehow turns romantic.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz Vodka
  • ½ oz Orange Liqueur
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • 1 oz Cranberry Juice

Method

  1. Shake vodka, orange liqueur, lime juice and cranberry juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Garnish: A lemon twist
