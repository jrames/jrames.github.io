It's a Wonderful Life 1946

5 real cocktails to drink with It's a Wonderful Life, each one matched to the film.

  • Drama: a classic
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Mulled WineOn screenMulled Claret · Mug Clarence follows his Flaming Rum Punch request with mulled wine, heavy on the cinnamon.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Double bourbonOn screenBourbon · Rocks glass Clarence's drinks; George orders a double bourbon

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon

Method

  1. Pour the bourbon into a serving glass. Ice is optional unless the scene specifies otherwise.
03 Bedford Falls EveIrish Coffee · Mug Hot coffee and cream warm George's Christmas Eve into something less hopeless after that bar stool night.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Irish Whiskey
  • 4 oz Coffee / Espresso
  • 1¾ oz Heavy Cream
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Method

  1. Add Irish whiskey, coffee and sugar to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Float cream on top.
04 Clarence's Wing PlanHot Brandy Sling · Mug Brandy and hot water make a steady little rescue for a town that spends the night deciding what George means.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

05 Potter's Office ExitWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Lemon sharpens the bourbon just enough for George's angry walk away from Potter's office.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
