It's a Wonderful Life 1946

5 real cocktails to drink with It's a Wonderful Life, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Drama: a classic
  • Fantasy: herbal and golden
  • Romance: pink and sparkling
01 Front-Porch WinterMulled Claret · Mug Warm spice and red wine fit the film’s snowy, home-for-the-holidays feeling.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Small-Town After HoursOld Fashioned · Rocks glass Its steady bourbon warmth and bittersweet edge match the film’s humble, midcentury American mood.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¼ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters, optional

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add bourbon, simple syrup and Angostura bitters (and orange bitters, if using).
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with an orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: An orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry

03 Snowday ComfortTom and Jerry · Mug This hot, creamy classic feels like a wraparound blanket for the movie’s coziest moments.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

04 Good-Hearted TearjerkerWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Bright citrus and whiskey give it the same sweet-and-sad balance as the story’s emotional turns.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
05 Warm-Glow Porch LightGold Rush · Rocks glass Honeyed bourbon keeps the comfort high while the lemon keeps it lively and hopeful.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice

Method

  1. Shake bourbon, honey syrup and lemon juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  3. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Garnish: A lemon twist
