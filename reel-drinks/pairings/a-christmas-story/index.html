A Christmas Story 1983

5 real cocktails to drink with A Christmas Story, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Old-School Holiday NightcapTom and Jerry · Mug A warm, creamy cooler that fits the film’s snowy, nostalgic, basement-and-kitchen holiday mood.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 After-Caroling ComfortHot Brandy Sling · Mug Its simple heat and brandy warmth echo the film’s snug, old-fashioned winter atmosphere.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

03 Tree-Lights in a GlassMulled Claret · Mug Spiced red wine feels like a quiet, glowing match for the story’s cozy Christmas color palette.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

04 Back-Porch Winter SipWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Bright and familiar, it matches the film’s plainspoken Midwestern feel without getting too fancy.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
05 Snow-Day EasygoingBourbon Collins · Collins glass This easy, soda-bright whiskey drink suits the film’s playful, unhurried holiday rhythm.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • 3 tsp Sugar
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • 4 oz Soda Water

Method

  1. Stir sugar and lemon juice in a shaker until the sugar begins to dissolve.
  2. Add bourbon and ice, then shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into an ice-filled Collins glass.
  4. Add soda water and stir gently.
