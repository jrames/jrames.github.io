National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation 1989

5 real cocktails to drink with National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Holiday Hearth PourMulled Claret · Mug Warm spice and ruby wine give it the same cozy, stocking-stuffed glow as a night in front of the tree.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Snow-Day Cuddle BowlTom and Jerry · Mug Its creamy, old-school warmth feels tailor-made for a sweet, retro winter evening.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

03 After-Decor Wind-DownHot Brandy Sling · Mug Simple, steamy, and comforting, it matches the film’s gentle holiday exhale.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

04 Late-Night Pajama PickWhite Russian · Rocks glass The soft cream and coffee notes make it a mellow, indulgent fit for a laid-back seasonal watch.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • 1 oz Coffee Liqueur
  • 1 oz Heavy Cream

Method

  1. Build vodka and coffee liqueur over ice.
  2. Float cream on top and stir if you like.
05 Bright Red Ribbon SipWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Its clean citrus snap keeps the mood lively while still feeling classic and wintry.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.

Director's Cut

Chicago Window Glow · New York Sour

Its deep red layer feels just right for the film's cozy, festive glow. A red wine float sits above a full-sized whiskey sour.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • 1 oz Simple Syrup (1:1 sugar to water)
  • ¼ oz Red Wine (Float separately; do not shake)

Method

  1. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  3. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
