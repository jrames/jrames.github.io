National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation 1989

5 real cocktails to drink with National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Tennessee WhiskeyOn screenBourbon · Rocks glass Clark's father credits Tennessee whiskey when his son asks how he survived family Christmases.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon

Method

  1. Pour the bourbon into a serving glass. Ice is optional unless the scene specifies otherwise.
02 No Saw Tree HuntMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for a family tree hunt deep in the woods with no saw and a huge tree.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

03 Overcooked TurkeyTom and Jerry · Mug Hot rum, brandy and batter, rich enough to make up for a turkey that collapses at the first cut.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

04 Locked in the AtticHot Brandy Sling · Mug Brandy, sugar and hot water for a dad stuck in the attic watching old home movies in whatever clothes he found.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

05 Greased Saucer SledMoscow Mule · Copper mug Vodka, ginger beer and lime, very fast, for a saucer sled coated in kitchen lubricant.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • ¾ oz Lime juice
  • 4 oz Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Fill a copper mug with ice and add vodka and lime juice.
  2. Top with ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with a lime slice.

Garnish: A lime slice
