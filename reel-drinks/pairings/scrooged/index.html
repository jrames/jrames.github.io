01 Midnight ManhattanManhattan · Cocktail glass Rye and vermouth cut through the Christmas-Eve cynicism like a producer’s tie on deadline.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: A cocktail cherry

Ledger Light · Black Manhattan

Its darker, gentler bitterness fits the wintry, bittersweet mood and feels right for a long night indoors. Averna replaces vermouth, with aromatic and orange bitters.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Amaro (Averna)
  • 1 dash Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, amaro, Angostura bitters and orange bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry.
02 Studio Exit StairwellPaper Plane · Coupe glass Bourbon, amaro, and Aperol keep this one sharp for the fluorescent maze behind the cameras.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Amaro (Nonino)
  • 1 oz Aperol
  • 1 oz Lemon juice

Method

  1. Shake bourbon, amaro, Aperol and lemon juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
03 Christmas Eve CurbsideMulled Claret · Mug Warm spiced wine belongs beside the curb snow and the city steam when the night gets mean.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

04 Upper West RooftopNew York Sour · Rocks glass The red wine float lands like a hard-earned soft spot after too many cold New York hours.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional
  • ½ oz Red Wine

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with lemon or orange zest with cherry.

Garnish: Lemon or orange zest with cherry

05 Morning After BroadcastBloody Mary · Highball glass Tomato, pepper, and hot sauce hit like an all-night call sheet and a studio headache.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Vodka
  • 3 oz Tomato Juice
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • 2 dashes Worcestershire
  • 1 dash Hot Sauce
  • 1 pinch Black Pepper
  • 1 pinch Salt

Method

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice and add vodka, tomato juice, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, black pepper and salt.
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with celery, Lemon Wedge.

Garnish: Celery, Lemon Wedge
