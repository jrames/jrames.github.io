02 Network BossManhattan · Cocktail glass Rye and sweet vermouth for a TV network boss who fires an employee the week of Christmas without blinking.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: A cocktail cherry

Ledger Light · Black Manhattan

Its darker, gentler bitterness fits the wintry, bittersweet mood and feels right for a long night indoors. Averna replaces vermouth, with aromatic and orange bitters.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Amaro (Averna)
  • 1 dash Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, amaro, Angostura bitters and orange bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry.
03 Live Christmas Eve BroadcastPaper Plane · Coupe glass Bourbon, amaro and Aperol in exact balance, for a live Christmas Eve broadcast that goes badly off script.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Amaro (Nonino)
  • 1 oz Aperol
  • 1 oz Lemon juice

Method

  1. Shake bourbon, amaro, Aperol and lemon juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
04 Claire's ShelterMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for an old girlfriend who spends Christmas running a homeless shelter.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

05 Mouse with AntlersNew York Sour · Rocks glass Bourbon sour with a red wine float for a mouse with tiny antlers and a boss who suggests staples.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional
  • ½ oz Red Wine

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with lemon or orange zest with cherry.

Garnish: Lemon or orange zest with cherry
