01 Russian vodkaOn screenVodka · Rocks glass Frank blames contaminated Russian vodka when his dead boss appears to him.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka

Method

  1. Pour the vodka into a serving glass. Ice is optional unless the scene specifies otherwise.
02 Christmas Eve at IBCManhattan · Cocktail glass Rye and vermouth keep it sharp, like Frank Cross racing a live broadcast from his office on Christmas Eve.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: A cocktail cherry

Rhinelander's Office · Black Manhattan

Averna makes it darker and more bitter, like Frank turning the boardroom into a holiday ambush. Averna replaces vermouth, with aromatic and orange bitters.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Amaro (Averna)
  • 1 dash Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, amaro, Angostura bitters and orange bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry.
03 Claire at the ShelterHot Toddy · Mug Honeyed and hot, the way Claire keeps going at the homeless shelter while Frank trips over his own cynicism.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick

04 Sidewalk Utility SpaceNew York Sour · Rocks glass That red wine cap turns the whole thing grim, like Herman's frozen corner under the sidewalk.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional
  • ½ oz Red Wine

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with lemon or orange zest with cherry.

Garnish: Lemon or orange zest with cherry

05 Taxi Driver PastPenicillin · Rocks glass Smoke and ginger cut through the sweetness, just like the Ghost of Christmas Past hauling Frank back to 1955.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Blended Scotch
  • ¼ oz Peated Scotch
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 2 slices Fresh Ginger

Method

  1. Gently muddle fresh ginger in a shaker.
  2. Add Scotch, lemon juice and honey syrup and shake hard with ice.
  3. Double-strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float peated Scotch on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with candied ginger.

Garnish: Candied ginger
