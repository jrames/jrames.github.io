White Christmas 1954

5 real cocktails to drink with White Christmas, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Romance: pink and sparkling
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
01 Hot Buttered RumOn screenHot Buttered Rum · Mug

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick

02 Snowy-night closerIrish Coffee · Mug Warm coffee, cream, and whiskey fit the film’s cozy, after-hours glow.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Irish Whiskey
  • 4 oz Coffee / Espresso
  • 1¾ oz Heavy Cream
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Method

  1. Add Irish whiskey, coffee and sugar to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Float cream on top.
03 Holiday party centerpieceEgg Nog · Collins glass Rich, pale, and festive, this is the kind of velvet-smooth drink that belongs in a cheerful winter gathering.

Ingredients

  • 1 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 4 oz Milk

Method

  1. Shake an egg, sugar, cognac, dark rum and milk hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

04 Old-school hearth warmerHot Brandy Sling · Mug Its simple brandy sweetness and hot serve suit the film’s midcentury holiday comfort.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

05 Postcarol comfort bowlTom and Jerry · Mug Spiced, creamy, and winter-bright, it feels like a classic Christmas season treat.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg
