White Christmas 1954

5 real cocktails to drink with White Christmas, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Romance: pink and sparkling
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Hot Buttered RumOn screenHot Buttered Rum · Mug

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick

02 General Waverly's PourEgg Nog · Collins glass Eggs and brandy keep the cold Vermont setbacks from turning into a full holiday mutiny.

Ingredients

  • 1 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 4 oz Milk

Method

  1. Shake an egg, sugar, cognac, dark rum and milk hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

03 Columbia Inn HearthHot Brandy Sling · Mug This one brings the fireplace; brandy and hot water suit the empty inn better than any crowd would.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

04 Pine Tree ServiceMulled Claret · Mug Red wine and mulling spice warm the dead-silent Vermont lobby while the snow still refuses to show up.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

05 Broadway SpotlightsFrench 75 · Flute Sparkling wine and gin keep the stage work crisp, bright, and just a little too polished for the snow delay.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 oz Sparkling Wine (Champagne)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
