5 real cocktails to drink with Elf, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy

01 Snowy Midtown SipperTom and Jerry · Mug Creamy and spiced, like a department-store break room after the city has chewed through your afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Rockefeller Tree NightMulled Claret · Mug Red wine and mulling spice turn that giant tree lot into something warmer than the sidewalks outside.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

03 Central Park SnowbanksMojito · Highball glass Mint and lime keep this bright, the way fresh snow does after the sleigh-bell chaos starts rolling.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz White Rum
  • ¾ oz Lime juice
  • 6 sprigs Fresh Mint
  • 2 tsp Sugar
  • Top Soda Water

Method

  1. Gently muddle mint, sugar and lime juice in a highball glass.
  2. Add white rum and fill with crushed ice.
  3. Top with soda water and stir gently.
  4. Garnish with sprigs of mint and slice of lime.

Garnish: Sprigs of mint and slice of lime

04 Toy Aisle RescueBee’s Knees · Coupe glass Honeyed gin sweetens the hard edges, handy for anyone bouncing from candy canes to chaos.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Honey Syrup
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ⅓ oz Orange juice, optional

Method

  1. Shake gin, honey syrup and lemon juice (and orange juice, if using) hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  3. Garnish with a lemon or orange zest.

Garnish: A lemon or orange zest

05 Apartment Heater SteamHot Toddy · Mug Honeyed whiskey is your answer when the city wind bites harder than the wrapping paper.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick
