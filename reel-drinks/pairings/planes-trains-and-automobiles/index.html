5 real cocktails to drink with Planes, Trains and Automobiles, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
01 Two Days to ThanksgivingBourbon Collins · Collins glass Bourbon and soda, tall and steady, for a man with one job: be in Chicago in time for dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • 3 tsp Sugar
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • 4 oz Soda Water

Method

  1. Stir sugar and lemon juice in a shaker until the sugar begins to dissolve.
  2. Add bourbon and ice, then shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into an ice-filled Collins glass.
  4. Add soda water and stir gently.
02 Diverted to WichitaMoscow Mule · Copper mug Ginger beer with a kick, for a flight that was supposed to land in Chicago and set down in Kansas instead.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • ¾ oz Lime juice
  • 4 oz Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Fill a copper mug with ice and add vodka and lime juice.
  2. Top with ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with a lime slice.

Garnish: A lime slice

03 Shower Curtain RingsWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Whiskey, lemon and a little sugar, for a salesman who never stops talking and never stops being kind.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
04 No Car in the SpacePaloma · Highball glass Grapefruit and salt, sharp enough for a man who has walked the whole lot and found no car in his space.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tequila
  • 1½ oz Grapefruit juice (fresh juice)
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • Top Soda Water
  • Pinch Salt

Method

  1. Add tequila, juices, syrup and salt to an ice-filled glass.
  2. Top with soda and stir.

Garnish: Grapefruit wedge

05 Wrong Side of the HighwayCuba Libre · Highball glass Rum and cola, cheap and cheerful, for the road trip where the car catches fire and they keep driving anyway.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz White Rum
  • 4 oz Cola
  • ¼ oz Lime juice

Method

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice and add white rum and lime juice.
  2. Top with cola and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with lime wedge.

Garnish: Lime wedge
