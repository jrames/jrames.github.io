Planes, Trains and Automobiles 1987

5 real cocktails to drink with Planes, Trains and Automobiles, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Diverted to WichitaIrish Coffee · Mug Hot coffee and Irish whiskey for a stranded traveler whose Chicago flight just landed in Kansas instead.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Irish Whiskey
  • 4 oz Coffee / Espresso
  • 1¾ oz Heavy Cream
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Method

  1. Add Irish whiskey, coffee and sugar to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Float cream on top.
02 Broken-Down TrainHot Brandy Sling · Mug Brandy and hot water for the long walk across a cold Missouri field after the train gives out.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

03 Two Days to ThanksgivingBourbon Collins · Collins glass Bourbon and soda, tall and steady, for a man with one job: be in Chicago in time for dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • 3 tsp Sugar
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • 4 oz Soda Water

Method

  1. Stir sugar and lemon juice in a shaker until the sugar begins to dissolve.
  2. Add bourbon and ice, then shake until chilled.
  3. Strain into an ice-filled Collins glass.
  4. Add soda water and stir gently.
04 One More Chair at DinnerTom and Jerry · Mug Rum, brandy and warm batter for a long way home that ends at a full table with one more chair.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

05 No Car in the SpaceMoscow Mule · Copper mug Vodka, ginger and lime, sharp enough for a man who walks the whole rental lot and finds no car.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • ¾ oz Lime juice
  • 4 oz Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Fill a copper mug with ice and add vodka and lime juice.
  2. Top with ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with a lime slice.

Garnish: A lime slice
