01 Plaza After MidnightNew York Sour · Rocks glass Whiskey sour with red wine floated on top, for a Christmas Eve office party in L.A. that ends in broken glass.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional
  • ½ oz Red Wine

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, simple syrup and lemon juice (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  4. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
  5. Garnish with lemon or orange zest with cherry.

Garnish: Lemon or orange zest with cherry

02 Tower Christmas PartyOld Fashioned · Rocks glass Bourbon, sugar and bitters for an office Christmas party high up a new L.A. tower, just before the uninvited guests arrive.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¼ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters, optional

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add bourbon, simple syrup and Angostura bitters (and orange bitters, if using).
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with an orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: An orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry

03 Barefoot New York CopManhattan · Cocktail glass Rye and sweet vermouth for a New York cop making fists with his toes in an executive washroom.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: A cocktail cherry

Director's Cut

Tower Shadow · Black Manhattan

Its deeper, drier profile suits the film's hard-edged night tension. Averna replaces vermouth, with aromatic and orange bitters.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Amaro (Averna)
  • 1 dash Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, amaro, Angostura bitters and orange bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry.
04 Vent Shaft CrawlBoulevardier · Rocks glass Bourbon, Campari and vermouth, bitter and cramped, for crawling through an air duct by the light of a lighter.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth

Method

  1. Stir bourbon, Campari and sweet vermouth with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
  3. Garnish with orange zest, optionally a lemon zest.

Garnish: Orange zest, optionally a lemon zest

05 Broken Glass FloorSazerac · Rocks glass Rye, absinthe and bitters, sharp as the broken glass a barefoot man has to cross anyway.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 dashes Peychaud's Bitters
  • Rinse Absinthe

Method

  1. Rinse a rocks glass with absinthe and discard the excess.
  2. Stir rye whiskey, simple syrup and Peychaud's bitters with ice until very cold.
  3. Strain into the glass.
  4. Garnish with lemon zest.

Garnish: Lemon zest
