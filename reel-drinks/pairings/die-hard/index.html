Die Hard 1988

5 real cocktails to drink with Die Hard, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Mystery: dark and bitter
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 After-Hours SteelManhattan · Cocktail glass Sharp rye and bitter vermouth match the film’s hard-edged, late-night tension and red-toned city glare.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: A cocktail cherry

Director's Cut

High-Rise Shadow · Black Manhattan

Its deeper, more bitter profile fits the film’s cold, tense mood and dark steel-and-glass setting. Averna replaces vermouth, with aromatic and orange bitters.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Amaro (Averna)
  • 1 dash Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, amaro, Angostura bitters and orange bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry.
02 Dust and BrassOld Fashioned · Rocks glass Its brown, no-nonsense whiskey profile feels like a bruised, old-school action night.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • ¼ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters
  • 1 dash Orange Bitters, optional

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add bourbon, simple syrup and Angostura bitters (and orange bitters, if using).
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with an orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: An orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry

03 Holiday GunmetalBoulevardier · Rocks glass Bourbon, Campari, and vermouth bring a bitter, classy bite that fits the wintry menace.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth

Method

  1. Stir bourbon, Campari and sweet vermouth with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
  3. Garnish with orange zest, optionally a lemon zest.

Garnish: Orange zest, optionally a lemon zest

04 Cold Glass ResolveSazerac · Rocks glass Lean rye and anise bite give it the sharp, merciless snap of a tense siege story.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 2 dashes Peychaud's Bitters
  • Rinse Absinthe

Method

  1. Rinse a rocks glass with absinthe and discard the excess.
  2. Stir rye whiskey, simple syrup and Peychaud's bitters with ice until very cold.
  3. Strain into the glass.
  4. Garnish with lemon zest.

Garnish: Lemon zest

05 City Block ShadowsVieux Carrè · Cocktail glass The layered rye-and-cognac depth suits the film’s gritty, adult, retro mood.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 1 tsp Bénédictine
  • 2 dashes Peychaud's Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, cognac, sweet vermouth, Bénédictine and Peychaud's bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with orange zest and maraschino cherry.

Garnish: Orange zest and maraschino cherry
