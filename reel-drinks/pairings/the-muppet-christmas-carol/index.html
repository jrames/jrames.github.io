The Muppet Christmas Carol 1992

5 real cocktails to drink with The Muppet Christmas Carol, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Same era
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • British roots
01 Christmas-Card WarmthMulled Claret · Mug Spiced red wine gives the whole night that candlelit, old-England holiday glow.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Parlor-Glass ComfortHot Brandy Sling · Mug A simple hot brandy serve feels like a fireside pause in a snug Victorian drawing room.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

03 Snowy-Doorstep IndulgenceEgg Nog · Collins glass Creamy, pale, and festive, it matches the storybook sweetness of a winter gathering.

Ingredients

  • 1 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 4 oz Milk

Method

  1. Shake an egg, sugar, cognac, dark rum and milk hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

04 Merriment by the HearthTom and Jerry · Mug Warm batter, spice, and rum bring the same old-fashioned holiday cheer as a crowded singalong.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

05 Lantern-Walk SipperHot Toddy · Mug Honeyed whiskey warmth suits the film’s chilly streets and tender, wintry mood.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick
