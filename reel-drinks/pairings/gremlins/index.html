01 Snowy Little TownTom and Jerry · Mug Hot rum, brandy and batter for a snowy little town where the carolers have no idea what's coming.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Three Simple RulesLast Word · Nick & Nora glass Gin, green Chartreuse and lime, bright green and easy to get wrong, like three simple rules about a new pet.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Gin
  • ¾ oz Green Chartreuse
  • ¾ oz Maraschino Liqueur
  • ¾ oz Lime juice

Method

  1. Shake gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur and lime juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry at the bottom of the cocktail.

Garnish: Cherry at the bottom of the cocktail

03 Chinatown Curio ShopMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for a traveling inventor bringing home a strange little present from a Chinatown shop.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

04 Mom's Kitchen DefenseBloody Mary · Highball glass Vodka, tomato and hot sauce for a mother defending her kitchen with a blender, a knife and a microwave.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Vodka
  • 3 oz Tomato Juice
  • ½ oz Lemon juice
  • 2 dashes Worcestershire
  • 1 dash Hot Sauce
  • 1 pinch Black Pepper
  • 1 pinch Salt

Method

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice and add vodka, tomato juice, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, black pepper and salt.
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with celery, Lemon Wedge.

Garnish: Celery, Lemon Wedge

05 Tavern TakeoverNegroni · Rocks glass Gin, Campari and vermouth, bitter and steady, for a bartender serving a bar full of smoking, drunken monsters.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add gin, Campari and sweet vermouth.
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with a half orange slice.

Garnish: A half orange slice

Director's Cut

Green Corridor Mischief · Green Negroni

Its vivid green glow suits the playful spooky energy and keeps the sip crisp. White bitter, blanc vermouth and green liqueurs turn the Negroni green.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Gin (London dry gin)
  • ¾ oz White Italian Bitter (Luxardo Bitter Bianco; not Campari or gentian liqueur)
  • ¾ oz Blanc Vermouth (Blanc / bianco, not dry or red vermouth)
  • ⅓ oz Green Chartreuse (Green herbal liqueur)
  • ½ oz Green Melon Liqueur (Green melon liqueur, such as Midori)

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add gin, white Italian bitter, blanc vermouth, green Chartreuse and green melon liqueur.
  2. Stir gently and garnish with a Galia melon wedge.
