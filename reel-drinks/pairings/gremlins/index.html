5 real cocktails to drink with Gremlins, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Horror: blood red and smoky

01 Holiday chaos in a mugMulled Claret · Mug Warm spice and red wine fit the wintry, mischievous holiday mood with a cozy-but-uneasy glow.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 After-midnight parlor warmthTom and Jerry · Mug Its creamy, spiced heat feels like a nostalgic winter night that could turn unruly at any moment.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

03 Little green troubleLast Word · Nick & Nora glass The vivid green color and sharp herbal bite echo the film’s playful, impish energy.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Gin
  • ¾ oz Green Chartreuse
  • ¾ oz Maraschino Liqueur
  • ¾ oz Lime juice

Method

  1. Shake gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur and lime juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
  3. Garnish with cherry at the bottom of the cocktail.

Garnish: Cherry at the bottom of the cocktail

04 Midnight warning lightNegroni · Rocks glass Bitter, red, and sleek, it matches the story’s darker edges and old-school urban holiday feel.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add gin, Campari and sweet vermouth.
  2. Stir briefly to combine.
  3. Garnish with a half orange slice.

Garnish: A half orange slice

Director's Cut

Pine-Toned Mischief · Green Negroni

Its bright green hue suits the playful, spooky edge. White bitter, blanc vermouth and green liqueurs turn the Negroni green.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Gin (London dry gin)
  • ¾ oz White Italian Bitter (Luxardo Bitter Bianco; not Campari or gentian liqueur)
  • ¾ oz Blanc Vermouth (Blanc / bianco, not dry or red vermouth)
  • ⅓ oz Green Chartreuse (Green herbal liqueur)
  • ½ oz Green Melon Liqueur (Green melon liqueur, such as Midori)

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice and add gin, white Italian bitter, blanc vermouth, green Chartreuse and green melon liqueur.
  2. Stir gently and garnish with a Galia melon wedge.
05 Snowy noir sipBoulevardier · Rocks glass This stirred whiskey drink brings a dressed-up, wintry bitterness that suits the film’s retro adult-leaning edge.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth

Method

  1. Stir bourbon, Campari and sweet vermouth with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
  3. Garnish with orange zest, optionally a lemon zest.

Garnish: Orange zest, optionally a lemon zest
