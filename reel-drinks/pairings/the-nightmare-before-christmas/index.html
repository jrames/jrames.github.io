The Nightmare Before Christmas 1993

5 real cocktails to drink with The Nightmare Before Christmas, each one matched to the film.

  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Spiral Hill TwilightEl Diablo · Highball glass Crème de cassis turns the whole thing purple, like moonlight on the hill before the sleigh takes off.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Tequila
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Crème de Cassis
  • Top Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Build tequila and lime over ice.
  2. Top with ginger beer.
  3. Drizzle cassis over the top so it sinks.
02 Pumpkin Patch LanternsHot Brandy Sling · Mug Brandy and hot water keep it simple, like lantern light flickering through the patch after the dogs start digging.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

03 Snowman Border CrossingHot Oaxacan Penicillin · Mug Mezcal and ginger bring smoke and bite, which is exactly what you want when the holiday gets hijacked.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Tequila (reposado)
  • ½ oz Mezcal
  • ½ oz Ginger Syrup
  • ¼ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add tequila, mezcal, ginger syrup, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel.

Garnish: Lemon wheel

04 Town Square DecemberMulled Claret · Mug Red wine and mulling spice warm up the whole square, even when the decorations are more bone than ribbon.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

05 Lock, Stock, And FirewoodHot Buttered Rum · Mug Butter and rum make a chewy little winter punch, like a workshop stocked by somebody with too much enthusiasm.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick
