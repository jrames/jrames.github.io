The Nightmare Before Christmas 1993

5 real cocktails to drink with The Nightmare Before Christmas, each one matched to the film.

  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Spiral Hill MoonEl Diablo · Highball glass Tequila, cassis and ginger beer, dark purple, for a curling hill under a full moon and a king tired of Halloween.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Tequila
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Crème de Cassis
  • Top Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Build tequila and lime over ice.
  2. Top with ginger beer.
  3. Drizzle cassis over the top so it sinks.
02 Doors in the TreesHot Brandy Sling · Mug Brandy, sugar and hot water for a forest clearing of trees with holiday doors carved into them.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

03 Sleigh Shot DownHot Oaxacan Penicillin · Mug Tequila, mezcal, ginger and honey, hot and smoky, for a borrowed sleigh shot out of the sky.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Tequila (reposado)
  • ½ oz Mezcal
  • ½ oz Ginger Syrup
  • ¼ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add tequila, mezcal, ginger syrup, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel.

Garnish: Lemon wheel

04 Explaining ChristmasMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for a town meeting where Christmas gets explained and everyone still hears Halloween.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

05 Three Little KidnappersHot Buttered Rum · Mug Dark rum and butter for three masked trick-or-treaters sent to kidnap a man in a red suit.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick
