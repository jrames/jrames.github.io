The Nightmare Before Christmas 1993

5 real cocktails to drink with The Nightmare Before Christmas, each one matched to the film.

  • Fantasy: herbal and golden
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Christmas Town WhimsyOrange Whip · Collins glass Creamy orange and rum for Jack’s first dazzled walk through Christmas Town; sugar never looked so suspicious.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz Orange juice
  • 1 oz White Rum
  • 1 oz Vodka
  • 2 oz Heavy Cream

Method

  1. Briefly blend the orange juice, white rum, vodka and heavy cream without ice until frothy.
  2. Fill a Collins glass with ice, pour in the mixture and stir briefly.
02 Coffin Sleigh NightHot Buttered Rum · Mug Butter and dark rum warm the chill after Jack sends his skeletal reindeer into the winter sky.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick

03 Forest Door CrossingHot Oaxacan Penicillin · Mug Smoky tequila and mezcal suit the circle of doors, when one wrong turn opens into holiday trouble.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Tequila (reposado)
  • ½ oz Mezcal
  • ½ oz Ginger Syrup
  • ¼ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add tequila, mezcal, ginger syrup, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel.

Garnish: Lemon wheel

04 Sandy Claws ReportGolden Dream · Coupe glass Orange cream and Galliano make a too-sweet broadcast for the Mayor, who still can’t explain what Jack has done.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Galliano
  • ¾ oz Orange Liqueur
  • ¾ oz Orange juice
  • ¼ oz Heavy Cream

Method

  1. Shake Galliano, orange liqueur, orange juice and cream hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
05 Halloween Town ParadeEl Diablo · Highball glass Purple cassis and ginger beer bring the pageant colors of Halloween Town, where every celebration leans a little wicked.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Tequila
  • ½ oz Lime juice
  • ½ oz Crème de Cassis
  • Top Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Build tequila and lime over ice.
  2. Top with ginger beer.
  3. Drizzle cassis over the top so it sinks.
