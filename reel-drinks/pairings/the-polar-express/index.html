The Polar Express 2004

5 real cocktails to drink with The Polar Express, each one matched to the film.

  • Same era
  • Fantasy: herbal and golden
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 North Pole NogEgg Nog · Collins glass Creamy and celebratory, like that first ride to the North Pole when Christmas Eve suddenly gets serious.

Ingredients

  • 1 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 4 oz Milk

Method

  1. Shake an egg, sugar, cognac, dark rum and milk hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Observation Car WarmthHot Buttered Rum · Mug Butter and rum keep the chill off while the observation car rolls along and everybody pretends this trip is normal.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick

03 Hot Chocolate StopVerte Chaud · Wine glass Green Chartreuse sneaks into the cocoa, a sly little jolt for the hot chocolate scene and its white winter steam.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Green Chartreuse
  • 6 oz Hot Chocolate
  • Top Heavy Cream

Method

  1. Add green Chartreuse and hot chocolate to a warmed wine glass and stir until dissolved.
  2. Float cream on top.
04 Frozen Lake ToddyHot Toddy · Mug Honeyed whiskey steadies you better than the throttle ever did on that frozen lake stretch.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick

05 Conductor's Cocoa RushTom and Jerry · Mug Rich, foamy, and a little old-fashioned, just like the train kitchen when hot chocolate starts running the show.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg
