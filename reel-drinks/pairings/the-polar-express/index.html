The Polar Express 2004

5 real cocktails to drink with The Polar Express, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Same era
  • Fantasy: herbal and golden
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Dancing WaitersTom and Jerry · Mug Hot rum, brandy and batter for dancing waiters pouring hot chocolate in the dining car.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Train Outside the HouseHot Buttered Rum · Mug Dark rum and butter for a steam train stopping outside the house on Christmas Eve.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick

03 Fire on the Train RoofHot Toddy · Mug Bourbon, honey and hot water for a campfire on top of a moving train and a stranger who might be a ghost.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick

04 Frozen Lake CrossingWhisky Highball · Highball glass Whisky, soda and lemon, cold and clear, for a train skating across a frozen lake.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Japanese Whisky
  • 4½ oz Soda Water
  • Lemons (peel), optional

Method

  1. Fill a chilled glass with ice and stir to chill it.
  2. Add whisky, then very cold soda down the side.
  3. Stir once, gently. Add a lemon peel.
05 Punched TicketBee’s Knees · Coupe glass Gin, honey and lemon for a golden ticket punched with letters that only make sense at the end.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Gin
  • ½ oz Honey Syrup
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ⅓ oz Orange juice, optional

Method

  1. Shake gin, honey syrup and lemon juice (and orange juice, if using) hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  3. Garnish with a lemon or orange zest.

Garnish: A lemon or orange zest
