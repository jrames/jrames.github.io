The Polar Express 2004

5 real cocktails to drink with The Polar Express, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Same era
  • Fantasy: herbal and golden
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 Train-Window Hot BatterTom and Jerry · Mug A creamy, old-school winter warmer that matches the film’s snug, glowing holiday mood.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Platform-Side ComfortHot Buttered Rum · Mug Spiced and mellow, it feels like a blanket of warmth against the cold, blue-white night.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 1 oz Hot Buttered Rum Batter
  • Top Hot Water

Method

  1. Add aged rum and hot buttered rum batter to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Top with hot water.
  3. Garnish with cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Cinnamon stick

03 Conductor’s Cabin SipHot Toddy · Mug Honeyed heat gives it the same reassuring, snow-day comfort as the film’s coziest moments.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Honey Syrup
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add bourbon, honey syrup and hot water to a warmed mug and stir until dissolved.
  2. Garnish with lemon wheel and cinnamon stick.

Garnish: Lemon wheel and cinnamon stick

04 Lantern-Glow WarmerHot Brandy Sling · Mug Simple and amber-hued, it suits the story’s candlelit, wintry glow without getting too heavy.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

05 Snowfall in a GlassMulled Claret · Mug Its spiced red-wine warmth fits the festive, cozy atmosphere and the sense of a holiday evening in motion.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice
