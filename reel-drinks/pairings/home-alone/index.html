Home Alone 1990

5 real cocktails to drink with Home Alone, each one matched to the film.

  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
  • Holiday: creamy and spiced
01 McCallister Christmas EveEgg Nog · Collins glass Brandy and milk for the night the family packs for Paris and the whole house tilts into holiday chaos.

Ingredients

  • 1 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 oz Cognac / Brandy
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • 4 oz Milk

Method

  1. Shake an egg, sugar, cognac, dark rum and milk hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a tall glass.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

02 Suburban Power OutageHot Brandy Sling · Mug Hot water and brandy warm you through the blown alarm clocks and the dark Chicago-suburb morning.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water in a warmed mug.
  2. Add the cognac and stir.
  3. Garnish with a twist of lemon peel and grated nutmeg.

Garnish: Lemon peel & grated nutmeg

03 Attic AftermathTom and Jerry · Mug Rum and batter turn the attic sulk into a frothy Christmas grumble, sweet enough for a truce.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Tom and Jerry Batter (about a quarter of a mug)
  • ¾ oz Dark / Aged Rum
  • ¾ oz Cognac / Brandy
  • 4 oz Hot Water

Method

  1. Add the batter, dark rum and cognac to a warmed mug and stir well.
  2. Fill with hot water and stir again.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.

Garnish: Grated nutmeg

04 Paris Departure EveningMulled Claret · Mug Lemons and mulling spice keep this red-wine pour busy while the family scrambles for an international getaway.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

05 Thrown-Out TicketWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Bourbon and lemon come sharp, like a plane ticket tossed out with the spilled milk while nobody counts heads.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
