Home Alone 1990

5 real cocktails to drink with Home Alone, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
01 Snowed-In WindowpanesMulled Claret · Mug Hot red wine and spices warm up a Chicago house when the temperature outside is doing its best to be rude.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Red Sweater MailboxCape Codder · Highball glass Cranberry tartness keeps things bright, like that suburban front lawn under fresh snow and too much holiday cheer.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Vodka
  • 4 oz Cranberry Juice

Method

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice and add vodka.
  2. Top with cranberry juice and stir. Squeeze in a lime wedge if you like.

Garnish: Lime wedge

03 Basement Trolley DashMoscow Mule · Copper mug Ginger bite and lime snap through the cold the way a mad sprint across an overstuffed house should.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • ¾ oz Lime juice
  • 4 oz Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Fill a copper mug with ice and add vodka and lime juice.
  2. Top with ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with a lime slice.

Garnish: A lime slice

04 Toy Train TensionWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Lemons and whiskey bring a sharp little jolt, which is exactly what your nerves want before the next household ambush.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
05 Paris Departure GateBellini · Flute Sparkling wine and peach keep it light for a quick getaway from one airport into another city entirely.

Ingredients

  • 3¼ oz Sparkling Wine (prosecco)
  • 1¾ oz Peach Purée (white peach)

Method

  1. Stir peach purée with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
