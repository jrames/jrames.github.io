Home Alone 1990

5 real cocktails to drink with Home Alone, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
01 Snowed-In House PartyMulled Claret · Mug Warm spice and red wine fit the cozy, wintry feeling of a home-bound holiday afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Kitchen-Table ComfortWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Its simple, tart profile feels like a sturdy American classic for a nostalgic, playful evening.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.

Chicago Window Red · New York Sour

A bright red finish gives this cozy sour a festive glow. A red wine float sits above a full-sized whiskey sour.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Bourbon
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • 1 oz Simple Syrup (1:1 sugar to water)
  • ¼ oz Red Wine (Float separately; do not shake)

Method

  1. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
  3. Float red wine on top, pouring gently over the back of a spoon.
03 Cranberry on the MantelCape Codder · Highball glass Bright red and easygoing, it matches the film's candy-color holiday look without getting fussy.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Vodka
  • 4 oz Cranberry Juice

Method

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice and add vodka.
  2. Top with cranberry juice and stir. Squeeze in a lime wedge if you like.

Garnish: Lime wedge

04 Holiday Ribbon RedJack Rose · Coupe glass Apple and lemon give it a sweet-tart sparkle that suits the film's festive, retro mood.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Apple Brandy
  • ¾ oz Grenadine
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice

Method

  1. Shake apple brandy, grenadine and lemon juice hard with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
05 After-Dinner Grown-Up PourManhattan · Cocktail glass Rye and vermouth bring a classic city polish that nods to the story's old-school American feel.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Method

  1. Stir rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with a cocktail cherry.

Garnish: A cocktail cherry
