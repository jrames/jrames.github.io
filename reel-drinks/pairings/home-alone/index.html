Home Alone 1990

5 real cocktails to drink with Home Alone, each one matched to the film.

  • Action: big and bold
  • Comedy: bright and easy
  • Family: bright and fruity
  • Same era
01 Left Behind in WinnetkaMulled Claret · Mug Red wine, sugar and spice for a big suburban house the morning after everyone flew to Paris without the youngest.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Red Wine
  • 4 tsp Sugar
  • ¼ oz Lemon juice
  • 1 Mulling Spices (3 cloves, a pinch of allspice and a small cinnamon stick)

Method

  1. Warm the red wine, sugar, lemon juice and spices in a small pan until it just boils.
  2. Strain into a warmed mug.
  3. Garnish with a lemon slice.

Garnish: Lemon slice

02 Red Brick HouseCape Codder · Highball glass Vodka and cranberry, as red as the brick house two burglars have picked for Christmas Eve.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Vodka
  • 4 oz Cranberry Juice

Method

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice and add vodka.
  2. Top with cranberry juice and stir. Squeeze in a lime wedge if you like.

Garnish: Lime wedge

03 Treehouse Zip LineMoscow Mule · Copper mug Vodka, ginger beer and lime, quick and cold, for a zip line from the top floor to the treehouse.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • ¾ oz Lime juice
  • 4 oz Ginger Beer

Method

  1. Fill a copper mug with ice and add vodka and lime juice.
  2. Top with ginger beer and stir gently.
  3. Garnish with a lime slice.

Garnish: A lime slice

04 Grocery Run AloneWhiskey Sour · Rocks glass Whiskey and lemon for an eight-year-old doing the grocery shopping alone and running off with an unpaid toothbrush.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Bourbon
  • ¾ oz Lemon juice
  • ¾ oz Simple Syrup
  • ½ oz Egg Whites, optional

Method

  1. If you're using egg white, first shake it with bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup without ice for 10 seconds.
  2. Shake bourbon, lemon juice and simple syrup (and egg white, if using) hard with ice.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.
05 Flight to ParisBellini · Flute Peach and bubbles for a flight to Paris and a mother realizing midway who isn't on board.

Ingredients

  • 3¼ oz Sparkling Wine (prosecco)
  • 1¾ oz Peach Purée (white peach)

Method

  1. Stir peach purée with ice until very cold.
  2. Strain into a chilled flute glass.
  3. Top with sparkling wine.
