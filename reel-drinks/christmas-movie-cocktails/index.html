Christmas Movie Cocktails

One drink for each holiday classic, picked to match the film's mood, era and colors. Every one is a real, tested recipe. Tap a film for its full lineup.

Die Hard 1988 Plaza After Midnight New York Sour Whiskey sour with red wine floated on top, for a Christmas Eve office party in L.A. that ends in broken glass. Elf 2003 Snowy Midtown Sipper Tom and Jerry Creamy and spiced, like a department-store break room after the city has chewed through your afternoon. Home Alone 1990 Snowed-In Windowpanes Mulled Claret Hot red wine and spices warm up a Chicago house when the temperature outside is doing its best to be rude. It's a Wonderful Life 1946 Potterville Porch Light Gold Rush Honeyed bourbon softens the edges, because this story keeps asking whether one good night can save a whole life. National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation 1989 Piano Room Hearth Hot Brandy Sling Brandy and hot water bring a calm spark to the family-room wreckage, if only for one civilized minute. Love Actually 2003 West End Toast French 75 Bubbles and lemon keep the party moving, even when the evening keeps slipping from merry to melancholy. The Holiday 2006 Beverly Hills Morning Ramos Fizz Creamy foam and orange flower water turn brunch into a polished little escape, even if your life is a mess. A Christmas Story 1983 Basement Air Gun Whiskey Sour Sharp lemon cuts through the sweetness, like a kid aiming too hard and learning consequences the hard way. Gremlins 1984 Kitchen Lights Out Last Word Green Chartreuse keeps the holiday mischief sharp, like a tiny creature watching from the shadows with perfect timing. The Polar Express 2004 Cabin Heat Stop Hot Buttered Rum Butter and rum steam up like the dining car after midnight, when the snow outside starts acting dramatic. The Muppet Christmas Carol 1992 Feast Table Finale Egg Nog Creamy and pale as fresh snow, this one sweetens the final reunion without losing a bit of holiday melancholy. The Nightmare Before Christmas 1993 Spiral Hill Twilight El Diablo Crème de cassis turns the whole thing purple, like moonlight on the hill before the sleigh takes off. Scrooged 1988 Midnight Manhattan Manhattan Rye and vermouth cut through the Christmas-Eve cynicism like a producer’s tie on deadline. Klaus 2019 Snowy Attic Letters Egg Nog Custard-thick and sweet, like sorting good deeds by lamplight while the whole town pretends not to need help.
