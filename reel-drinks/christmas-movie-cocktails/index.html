Christmas Movie Cocktails

One drink for each holiday classic, picked to match the film's mood, era and colors. Every one is a real, tested recipe. Tap a film for its full lineup.

Die Hard 1988 After-Hours Steel Manhattan Sharp rye and bitter vermouth match the film’s hard-edged, late-night tension and red-toned city glare. Elf 2003 Holiday mug, over-the-top cheer Tom and Jerry Warm, creamy, and a little old-school, this feels like a living-room snow day in a glass. Home Alone 1990 Snowed-In House Party Mulled Claret Warm spice and red wine fit the cozy, wintry feeling of a home-bound holiday afternoon. It's a Wonderful Life 1946 Small-Town After Hours Old Fashioned Its steady bourbon warmth and bittersweet edge match the film’s humble, midcentury American mood. National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation 1989 After-Decor Wind-Down Hot Brandy Sling Simple, steamy, and comforting, it matches the film’s gentle holiday exhale. Love Actually 2003 Candlelit Sparkler French 75 Its crisp bubbles and bright citrus match the film’s polished, hopeful holiday glow. The Holiday 2006 Brunch-in-the-sun pick Mimosa Its sunny citrus lift suits the film’s easygoing, feel-good warmth and daytime glow. A Christmas Story 1983 Back-Porch Winter Sip Whiskey Sour Bright and familiar, it matches the film’s plainspoken Midwestern feel without getting too fancy. Gremlins 1984 Little green trouble Last Word The vivid green color and sharp herbal bite echo the film’s playful, impish energy. The Polar Express 2004 Platform-Side Comfort Hot Buttered Rum Spiced and mellow, it feels like a blanket of warmth against the cold, blue-white night. The Muppet Christmas Carol 1992 Snowy-Doorstep Indulgence Egg Nog Creamy, pale, and festive, it matches the storybook sweetness of a winter gathering. The Nightmare Before Christmas 1993 Purple Mischief El Diablo The dark cassis and ginger snap the whole glass into a mischievous purple glow that fits the film’s playful spookiness. Scrooged 1988 Bitter Little Miracle Paper Plane Its bright citrus and herbal bite match the movie’s dark playfulness and fast, wry energy. Klaus 2019 Quiet Night Watch Hot Toddy This gentle, honeyed warmer fits the film’s bittersweet tenderness and snowy hush. White Christmas 1954 Snowy-night closer Irish Coffee Warm coffee, cream, and whiskey fit the film’s cozy, after-hours glow.
