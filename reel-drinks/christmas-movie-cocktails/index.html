Christmas Movie Cocktails

One drink for each holiday classic, picked to match the film's mood, era and colors. Every one is a real, tested recipe. Tap a film for its full lineup.

Die Hard 1988 Plaza After Midnight New York Sour Whiskey sour with red wine floated on top, for a Christmas Eve office party in L.A. that ends in broken glass. Elf 2003 Syrup on Spaghetti Tom and Jerry Hot rum, brandy and sweet batter, about as subtle as spaghetti covered in maple syrup. Home Alone 1990 Left Behind in Winnetka Mulled Claret Red wine, sugar and spice for a big suburban house the morning after everyone flew to Paris without the youngest. It's a Wonderful Life 1946 Honeymoon Money Gold Rush Bourbon, honey and lemon for honeymoon money handed over to keep a bank run from closing the Building and Loan. National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation 1989 Locked in the Attic Hot Brandy Sling Brandy, sugar and hot water for a dad stuck in the attic watching old home movies in whatever clothes he found. Love Actually 2003 Musicians in the Pews French 75 Gin, lemon and bubbles for a church wedding where hidden musicians stand up in the pews. The Holiday 2006 Life as a Trailer Ramos Fizz Gin, cream and egg white, fluffy and dramatic, for a woman who narrates her own life like a movie trailer. A Christmas Story 1983 Frozen Flagpole Whiskey Sour Whiskey and lemon with a sharp bite, like a tongue frozen to a schoolyard flagpole. Gremlins 1984 Three Simple Rules Last Word Gin, green Chartreuse and lime, bright green and easy to get wrong, like three simple rules about a new pet. The Polar Express 2004 Train Outside the House Hot Buttered Rum Dark rum and butter for a steam train stopping outside the house on Christmas Eve. The Muppet Christmas Carol 1992 A Very Small Goose Egg Nog Brandy, rum, egg and milk for a small family dinner where everyone shares a very small goose. The Nightmare Before Christmas 1993 Spiral Hill Moon El Diablo Tequila, cassis and ginger beer, dark purple, for a curling hill under a full moon and a king tired of Halloween. Scrooged 1988 Network Boss Manhattan Rye and sweet vermouth for a TV network boss who fires an employee the week of Christmas without blinking. Klaus 2019 One Letter, One Toy Egg Nog Brandy, rum, egg and milk, rich and generous, for children learning that one letter can bring a toy. White Christmas 1954 Hot Buttered Rum Hot Buttered Rum
