Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Reel Drinks app ("Reel Drinks", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Reel Drinks pairs movies with cocktail recipes. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • No account, no name, no email. We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • Your Back Bar, saved pairings, ratings and notes stay on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • To find films and drinks, the app sends film searches and the film you pick to our server. These aren't linked to you.
  • If you scan your bar, the photos go to our server and an AI service to identify the bottles, then are thrown away.

What stays on your device

The bottles in your Back Bar, the pairings you save to My Tapes, your drink and movie ratings, your notes, and the list of drinks you've recently been shown are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, your Back Bar and My Tapes are also stored in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, so they sync across your own devices. We can't see your iCloud data.

Your Back Bar is not sent to our server to work out pairings: the app checks which drinks you can make on your device. (If we ever have to switch the app to its older backup pairing method during a service problem, the list of ingredient types on your Back Bar is sent with the film request, without brands or anything that identifies you, and isn't stored.)

Finding films

When you search for a film, the app sends the text you type to our server, which looks it up in Wikidata, the free public database of films. To answer repeat searches faster, search text and results may be cached for up to seven days and appear briefly in our server logs. They are not linked to you or your device.

The New Releases shelf is loaded straight from Apple's public top-movies chart, so Apple receives that request as it does for any website.

Drink pairings

When you open a film, the app asks our server for that film's drinks. The request names the film, not you. The first time anyone opens a particular film, our server sends public facts about it (such as its title, year, genre and setting) to OpenAI, which helps choose and name drinks from our own recipe catalog. The result is saved and shown to everyone who opens that film later. No personal information is sent to OpenAI for pairings.

Scan Your Bar (camera and photos)

Scan Your Bar is optional. It uses your camera, or photos you pick from your library, only when you choose to scan. Before a photo leaves your device, the app shrinks it and removes location and other metadata. The photo is then sent to our server, which passes it to OpenAI to recognize the bottles, and the bottles it finds come back to you to review.

  • We don't store or log your photos. Our server logs only counts, such as how many bottles were found.
  • We ask OpenAI not to store the request. Under OpenAI's API data policy, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models, and it may be kept for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse.
  • The app's working copies of your photos are deleted when you finish or discard a scan, or the next time the app opens.

Avoid scanning photos with people or personal documents in them.

Our servers and your IP address

Our server runs on Cloudflare. Like any internet service, it receives your device's IP address with each request. We use it only to stop any one device from sending too many requests, and we store it only as a scrambled, one-way code that is deleted within minutes. The app also downloads its settings and recipe catalog updates from our server; those requests contain nothing about you.

Analytics

To see which features are used and where things go wrong, Reel Drinks sends anonymous usage signals (for example, "a pairing was saved" or "a scan finished") to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Signals contain only general details such as rough counts and times (for example, "4 to 9 bottles"). They never include film titles, your searches, what's in your Back Bar, your notes or photos, your name or email, or an advertising identifier, and they aren't used to track you across other apps or websites.

What we don't do

Reel Drinks doesn't require an account, doesn't ask for your name or email, doesn't use advertising identifiers, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Age

Reel Drinks shows alcoholic cocktail recipes and is intended only for adults of legal drinking age where they live. It is not directed to children and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Please drink responsibly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app adds in-app purchases or new features. Material changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
