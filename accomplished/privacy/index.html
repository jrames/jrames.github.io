How your data is handled

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Accomplished app and the Accomplished pages at jefframes.com (together, “Accomplished,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handle information. Accomplished is designed to keep your work history under your control while clearly identifying the features that use cloud processing.

Information you add to Accomplished

The app stores the accomplishments you enter or import, dates, generated summaries and reports, preferences, and related organizational information needed to provide its features. Depending on the type of data, it is stored on your device or in your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit so it can remain available across your devices.

We do not operate a database that stores your accomplishment history or generated reports. Data stored in iCloud is handled under your Apple account and Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

AI processing

Accomplished can use three different kinds of AI processing. The route depends on the feature, your device and operating system, model availability, and the choices presented in the app.

On-device models

When an on-device Apple model is used, the submitted text and generated result are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

Apple Private Cloud Compute

Some features may use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute (“PCC”). In that case, the text needed for the request is sent to Apple for processing. Apple states that data sent to PCC is used only to fulfill the request and is not stored. PCC is governed by Apple’s applicable terms and privacy practices.

Remote reports

Optional features such as performance-review drafts and resume bullets may send the content you select to our Cloudflare Worker and then to OpenAI’s API. A request may include accomplishment or summary text, dates or timeframe labels, and optional guidance such as a job title, tone, or intended audience.

Our Worker processes these requests in transit and does not write their contents or generated responses to a database or object store. Responses are marked not to be cached, and requests to OpenAI use store: false. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default. However, unless Zero Data Retention is enabled for the applicable OpenAI project, OpenAI may retain prompts, responses, and related metadata in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, or longer when legally required. Cloudflare and OpenAI may also process technical and security metadata needed to operate and protect their services.

Do not include information in an AI request that you do not want processed by the provider identified for that feature.

GitHub and Linear integrations

If you choose to connect GitHub or Linear, Accomplished uses the authorization you grant to retrieve relevant work activity, such as merged pull-request titles or completed issue titles. The app communicates directly with GitHub or Linear for these imports. Access and refresh tokens are stored in your device Keychain and are not sent to our servers.

Disconnecting an integration removes its stored authorization tokens from the device. Work items already imported into Accomplished remain until you delete them. GitHub and Linear handle your information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive your payment-card details. Accomplished uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and determine Pro access. RevenueCat receives purchase and receipt information, basic app and device information, and a randomly generated anonymous App User ID. Accomplished does not send RevenueCat your name or email address.

Website analytics

The Accomplished product pages use GoatCounter to measure aggregate visits, such as page paths, referrers, browser and operating-system categories, approximate country, language, and screen size. GoatCounter does not use cookies or advertising identifiers and states that it does not store IP addresses or full user-agent strings on disk. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web-server information under its own privacy policy.

Diagnostics and tracking

Accomplished does not contain advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Apple may provide crash or diagnostic information if you choose to share analytics with app developers through your device settings.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • You can delete accomplishment data in the app and disconnect GitHub or Linear in Settings.
  • Deleting the app does not necessarily delete data already synced to iCloud. You can manage iCloud data through Apple’s settings and controls.
  • Our remote-report service does not intentionally retain report content after returning the response. Service providers may retain limited information as described above and in their own policies.
  • Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records as needed to provide subscriptions, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and maintain transaction history.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control. Because the app does not require an account and RevenueCat uses an anonymous identifier, we may need information from your device or a purchase record to locate a relevant record. Contact us for help.

Security

We use platform security features and encrypted network connections intended to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

Accomplished is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International processing

Apple, Cloudflare, OpenAI, RevenueCat, GitHub, Linear, GoatCounter, and their service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their respective safeguards and legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Accomplished changes. Material updates will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to jrames@gmail.com.
