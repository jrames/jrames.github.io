Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Box Tracker free?

Yes. You can add unlimited boxes, rooms, items, QR labels, photos, and family members for free. The $9.99 Pro unlock adds Instant Lookup and Unpack Mode.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is one purchase that you keep forever, with no renewal or annual fee.

How do QR labels work?

Each box gets a unique QR code. Print it, attach it to the box, and scan it later to jump directly to that box’s contents.

Where is my move data stored?

Your move data stays on your devices and is backed up to your private iCloud storage. If you share a move, its data is stored in shared iCloud storage that is available only to the people you invite. Your move data is never stored on our servers.

Can my family help with the move?

Yes. Share the move through iCloud so everyone can add boxes and check off items together.
