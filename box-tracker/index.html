Questions, answered

// BEFORE YOU TAPE UP BOX ONE //

Is Box Tracker free?

Yes — packing is free: unlimited boxes, rooms, items, QR labels, photos, and family sharing. A one-time $9.99 Pro unlock adds Instant Lookup search and Unpack Mode.

Is it a subscription?

No. Never. Pro is a single purchase you keep forever — this move, the storage unit, and the next move. Most moving apps charge $20+ every year for a tool you need for six weeks.

How do QR labels work?

Every box gets its own QR code. Print it, stick it on the box, and later scan it with your camera to jump straight to that box's contents — no opening, no digging.

Can my family help?

Yes — share the move through iCloud and everyone can add boxes and check off items together. Packing the kitchen while someone else does the garage actually works.
